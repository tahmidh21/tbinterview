About This Model ▼
  • The model is a RandomForestClassifier trained on approximately 1,000 patient symptom records.
  • Risk labels (Low / Medium / High) were derived from symptom count since the dataset had no ground-truth diagnosis labels.
  • The training data was imbalanced (~15% Low, ~74% Medium, ~11% High).
  • The model is highly reliable at identifying Medium-risk cases but has lower recall for Low and High extremes.
  • This prototype demonstrates full-stack integration of machine learning into a web application.

What Drives This Model's Predictions
